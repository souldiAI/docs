title: "Get started"
description: "Install Souldi: Virtual Try-On & Size on your Shopify store and put your first product live."

---

Souldi prepares your products in the background, so you review rather than build.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and choose a plan">
    1. Install **Souldi: Virtual Try-On & Size** from the Shopify App Store.
    2. Shopify shows the four plans: Starter ($19.90, no included renders, no trial), Boutique ($100, 6,500 renders), Growth ($350, 23,000 renders) and Flagship ($750, 50,000 renders). Every plan has the same features. Extra renders are $0.03 each.
    3. Approve the plan in Shopify's own window. The app opens on **Home**, with a short setup checklist.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the try-on button to your product page">
    1. In your Shopify admin, open **Online Store → Themes → Customize**.
    2. Open a product template and add the **Souldi** block.
    3. Save. The button shows on a product only once you add that product (next step), and the theme editor shows a short note in its place until then.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your products">
    1. Open **Try-on** in the app. On a new store it opens on **Add products**.
    2. Choose how to add: **Products** (pick them), **A collection**, or **A product type**.
    3. Choose **Prepare**. Your products appear under **Getting ready → Preparing**. This can take up to 30 minutes. Reload to see progress. Nothing changes on your storefront yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and accept each product">
    1. When a product reaches **Waiting for your review**, choose **Open**.
    2. You see what Souldi prepared: the photos it picked, the size chart it read, and the try-on made on a Model.
    3. Choose **Accept and go live**. The product is now **Live** and its button appears on its page (if Shopify is slow to take the change, within 15 minutes).

    You can accept several products at once from the review queue. If you reject one, you can have Souldi **Refine try-on**, or **Switch off**.

    <Note>
      If Souldi could not prepare a product, it is listed under **Couldn't be prepared**. Open it to **Retry**, or **Go live without preparation**.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the sizes">
    Open a live product's review to see its size chart. A product with no chart can still go live. To fill many charts at once, use the **Size Sheet**: download a spreadsheet, fill in each size's measurements, upload it back.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See what a shopper sees">
    1. Open a live product on your storefront and press the try-on button.
    2. Sign in with an email code and upload a photo.
    3. The result opens in a window labelled **AI-generated**, with the recommended size and **Add to cart**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep an eye on usage">
    **Home** and **Settings → Plan and usage** show the renders used this month. On Boutique, Growth and Flagship they show how much of your allowance is left. On Starter they read "N renders this month, no renders included" and what has been charged so far.
  </Step>
</Steps>
