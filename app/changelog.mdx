<Update label="14 November 2026" description="AI label, Starter plan, new app name">
  - **AI-generated label.** Every try-on result shoppers see now says AI-generated, in their language, without covering the picture, the size or Add to cart.
  - **Starter plan.** A fourth plan, $19.90 a month, with no included renders: every render is $0.03 from the first. No trial. Boutique, Growth and Flagship are unchanged.
  - **App name.** The app is now called Souldi: Virtual Try-On & Size.
</Update>

<Update label="13 November 2026" description="Outfit Combinator on your storefront">
  - **Outfit Combinator on your storefront.** A **Build an outfit** link (a block you can place anywhere, and a link under the try-on button) opens a page where shoppers mix your live products into a look and add the whole look to the Cart. Souldi switches it on per store.
  - **"Is this helping you decide?"** After a try-on, shoppers can say whether it helped. They are asked at most once a day.
</Update>

<Update label="12 November 2026" description="Spanish everywhere">
  - **Spanish everywhere.** The shopper window, the sign-in email, the phone-upload page, the theme block and the app itself now speak Spanish as well as English. Shoppers get their store's language; each staff member gets their own Shopify admin's language.
  - **Clear errors.** A shopper never sees a technical error message; every failure is worded for them.
</Update>

<Update label="6–9 November 2026" description="Redesigned try-on window and review queue">
  - **A redesigned try-on window.** On desktop the picture fills one side and the decision (size, Add to cart) sits beside it. On a phone it is a bottom sheet. Front and back views, "No fitting size" and one-size products are handled.
  - **Always a try-on to review.** Every prepared product reaches your review with a try-on picture, and **Accept** is never offered without one (**Retry** is offered instead).
  - **Review queue actions.** Run try-on, **Accept and go live** and reject on many products at once.
  - **Models.** Your Models are the people Souldi renders your products on when you review them, and Souldi's two Sample Models are marked as Souldi's.
  - **Size charts read correctly.** Charts are read against the product's own sizes, and shoppers are told when none of a product's sizes fits them.
</Update>
