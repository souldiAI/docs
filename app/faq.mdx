## Plans and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What plans are there?" defaultOpen>
    Four. Every plan has the same features, so you choose by how many renders you need.

    | Plan | Price a month | Renders included | Extra renders |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Starter | $19.90 | None | $0.03 each, from the first |
    | Boutique | $100 | 6,500 | $0.03 each |
    | Growth | $350 | 23,000 | $0.03 each |
    | Flagship | $750 | 50,000 | $0.03 each |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a render?">
    One try-on picture made for a shopper. A picture of several pieces worn together is still one render. Preparing your products, and the try-on you review before accepting one, are never charged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which plan should I pick?">
    Starter suits a small store or a first month: it has no included renders, so you pay $19.90 plus $0.03 for each render. Boutique includes 6,500 renders a month, so a busier store may prefer it. Boutique, Growth and Flagship include renders at about $0.015 each when you use the whole allowance; use less and each costs more. Starter is $0.03 a render plus $19.90 a month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Boutique, Growth and Flagship have a 7-day free trial. **Starter has no trial**, and its renders are charged from the first one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much will Starter cost in a busy month?">
    Starter is $19.90 a month plus $0.03 for each render, so the bill grows with use. Your renders this month show on **Home** and under **Settings → Plan and usage**. If you expect heavy use, Boutique includes 6,500 renders a month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I pay?">
    Only through your Shopify invoice. Souldi never asks for a card, and there is no other way to pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change plan?">
    In the app, **Settings → Plan and usage** links to Shopify's own plan page. Switching is done and approved there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setting up

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a developer?">
    No. Install the app, add the Souldi block to your product page in the theme editor, then choose which products offer try-on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do all my products get try-on?">
    Only the ones you choose. Each product is prepared first and goes live only by your say-so: you accept it, or you choose to go live without preparation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Souldi do when it prepares a product?">
    It sorts the product's photos (each colour's front and back), reads its size chart, and makes a try-on picture for you to check on a Model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I take a product off?">
    Yes. In **Try-on**, tick it under **Live** and choose **Switch off**. The button disappears from its page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What shoppers see

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does a shopper do?">
    They press the try-on button on a product page, sign in with a code sent to their email, and upload a photo once. After that they see themselves in the product, with a recommended size and an **Add to cart** button.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the result marked as AI?">
    Yes. Every try-on result says **AI-generated**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can shoppers use their phone?">
    Yes. They can scan a QR code to upload a photo from their phone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages do shoppers see?">
    English and Spanish: the language the shopper is browsing your store in. Anything else shows English.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can shoppers build outfits?">
    The Outfit Combinator is on request: ask Souldi to switch it on for your store, and a **Build an outfit** link then takes shoppers to a page where they mix pieces into a look. They choose sizes and add the whole look to the Cart in one step. The page is in English for now, whatever language the shopper uses elsewhere.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where are images stored?">
    Images are stored in the EU. A shopper's photo and body data are private: they are not shared with the store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What language is the app and support in?">
    The app speaks English and Spanish, following each staff member's Shopify language. Support is in English and Spanish.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can shoppers delete their photos?">
    Yes. A shopper can view and delete their data, and delete their account, from their profile in the try-on window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I get from shoppers who use try-on?">
    The verified email of each shopper who used it on your store, listed in the app under **Settings**, with the date it was first seen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
